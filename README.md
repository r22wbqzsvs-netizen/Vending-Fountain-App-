## Installation Guide
1. Unpack the Vending Fountain.
2. Install the water dispenser in a sturdy location.
3. Connect the power supply to a suitable outlet.
4. Connect the water source to the dispenser.
5. Power on the unit and perform an initial check.

## Troubleshooting Guide
### Power Issues
- **Problem:** Unit does not power on.
  - **Solution:** Check the power supply connection. Ensure the outlet is functional.

### Water Dispensing Issues
- **Problem:** No water dispensed.
  - **Solution:** Check for blockages in the water line or ensure the water source is connected properly.

### Touchscreen Problems
- **Problem:** Touchscreen unresponsive.
  - **Solution:** Restart the unit. Ensure the touchscreen is clean and free from obstructions.

### Intermittent Power Loss
- **Problem:** Unit powers on and off randomly.
  - **Solution:** Check power supply integrity. Ensure all cables are firmly connected and not damaged.
